As part of the **[DesiRE project](https://idea-lab.uni-graz.at/en/research-projects/desire/)**, we are developing a large-scale simulation framework that leverages Large Language Models (LLMs) to create scenario simulations of Reddit-like and Twitter-like social media platforms.
