## Computational Neuroscience

In my PhD, I worked on developing and validating physics-inspired models for large-scale brain activity. We find that coarse-sampled signals of neuronal activity are prone to spurious correlations and autocorrelated measures of neuronal activity should preferably use spiking signals.
